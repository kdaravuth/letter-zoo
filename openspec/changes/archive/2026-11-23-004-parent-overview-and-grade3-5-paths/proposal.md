# Proposal: 004-parent-overview-and-grade3-5-paths

**PIN-Gated Parent Progress Dashboard & Grades 3–5 Geometry and Fractions/Decimals Paths**

- **From**: Single 10-lesson Early Foundations path (Grades 1–2) with no dedicated caregiver progress dashboard.
- **To**: Three unrestricted Khmer learning paths adding Grades 3–5 **Geometry (*ធរណីមាត្រ និងផ្ទៃក្រឡា*)** and **Fractions & Decimals (*ប្រភាគ និងចំនួនទសភាគ*)** (6 new validated JSON lessons), plus a PIN/math-gated **Parent Progress Dashboard** (`/parent`) showing plain-Khmer strengths, practice needs, and weekly rhythm per child.
- **Reason**: Directly addresses Cambodia's 35% Grade 6 geometry accuracy and late-primary fraction/decimal conceptual gaps while giving Cambodian parents clear, calm visibility on shared family devices.
- **Impact**: Adds two new capabilities (`parent-progress-overview`, `multi-path-curriculum`) without locking paths by grade or adding any punitive metrics.
