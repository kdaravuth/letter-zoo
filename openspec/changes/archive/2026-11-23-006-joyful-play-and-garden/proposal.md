# Proposal: 006-joyful-play-and-garden

**Joyful Tactile Math Play Studio, Living Khmer Garden & Musical Micro-Celebrations**

- **From**: Functional, calm lesson engine with static feedback panels and no free-play sandbox or living visual mastery world.
- **To**: Three child-delighting, non-manipulative interactive experiences:
  1. Ascending pentatonic xylophone audio notes on manipulative steps, a friendly SVG companion mascot (*កូនកង្កែបឆ្លាត*), and reduced-motion-safe floating lotus-petal celebrations inside `<LessonPlayer />`.
  2. **សួនគណិត (`/garden`)** — a living Cambodian mango orchard & lotus pond driven by real completed lessons/puzzles, where kids tap mangoes, blooming lotus flowers, butterflies, and a counting frog.
  3. **លេង និងស្វែងយល់ (`/play`)** — an interactive Khmer math playground featuring a **Cambodian Market Stall** (packing fruit orders + counting Riel notes) and a **Krama Pattern Weaver** (weaving & musically playing back `AB`/`ABB`/`ABC`/`AABB` patterns).
- **Reason**: Deepens intrinsic curiosity, tactile delight, and cultural pride for Cambodian children aged 6–12 while strictly preserving calm pedagogy (zero timers, zero loot boxes, zero virtual storefronts, zero `<foreignObject>`, WCAG 2.2 AA).
- **Impact**: Adds the `joyful-play-and-garden` capability and enriches existing lesson interactions without changing lesson JSON contracts.
