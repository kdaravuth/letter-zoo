How it works

  • Each letter follows real stroke order: start at the green dot, follow the arrow.
  • The child can lift a finger and carry on from where they stopped.
  • Stars show how neatly they stayed on the path: every finished letter gets at least one.
  • Finishing a letter hatches its animal. Doing both the capital and the small letter gives that friend a gold badge.
  • Treat Time: after each letter, the child drags a treat to the animal. Three treats make best friends and earn the animal a crown in the zoo.
  • Progress is saved on this device only.
  • The Champions board is public. Kids may add a first name (letters only, no surname); it shows as “Dara the Lion”. Leave it empty for a fun name like “Sunny Lion 27”.
  • The Our world tab counts visits anonymously: a random number made on this device, the time-zone city and the country. No names, photos, voices or IP addresses are kept.
